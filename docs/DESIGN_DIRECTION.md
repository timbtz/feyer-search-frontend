# Search map design

Reference inspected 21 November 2026: [Aionic](https://www.aioniclabs.ai/) and its public [Team & Jobs Notion page](https://aioniclabs.notion.site/team-careers). The homepage uses large, tightly set sans-serif headlines, quiet monochrome fields, fine technical lines, mono annotations and restrained orange highlights. Its scroll sequence introduces the problem, connects sources and then explains decisions. The public Notion page uses short concrete explanations and separates research from engineering responsibilities. Its typography, logo, media and proprietary assets are not copied here.

Feyer applies that visual hierarchy to the actual search: baseline, independent research directions, candidate revisions, bounded numerical refinement, branch stopping, combination and exact confirmation. Research names lead; operational IDs appear on inspection. The primary map is a workflow guide containing recorded evidence, not a reconstruction of missing scientific ancestry. Dashed guide lines describe intended progression. Revision markers are chronological branch membership, not parent edges. The separate Exact evidence DAG retains explicit scientific parents, multi-parent combinations, evaluations, and missing-data notices.

The first view shows the three most recent directions, prioritizing a worker only when its recorded status is active and not stale; all directions remain available. Numerical campaign outputs are excluded from this research grouping and shown in the refinement band. The displayed time is the latest full observation on that branch, not its best candidate or a cross-host ranking. Single observations, screen runs, rejected checks, and records without branch metadata remain distinct. No final solution is automatically inferred.
